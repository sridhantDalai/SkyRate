# ✈️ SkyRate

### AI-Powered Airfare Intelligence & Prediction Platform

> **Understand the fare. Predict the trend. Book smarter.**

SkyRate is an intelligent airfare analytics platform designed to make flight pricing more **transparent, predictable, and explainable**.

Instead of simply displaying ticket prices, SkyRate analyzes airfare trends alongside **fuel prices, aviation data, economic indicators, CPI, and historical patterns** to identify anomalies, predict price movements, and explain the factors driving changes in airfare.

---

## 🚀 Why SkyRate?

Airfare can change dramatically due to multiple interacting factors — from fuel costs and airline capacity to seasonal demand and broader economic conditions.

SkyRate brings these signals together into a single intelligent platform.

### SkyRate helps answer:

* 📈 **Will the airfare increase or decrease?**
* 🚨 **Is the current fare unusual?**
* ⛽ **How are fuel prices affecting ticket prices?**
* 📊 **How does airfare movement compare with CPI?**
* 🔎 **Why did the price change?**
* 🔔 **When should a user consider booking?**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Intelligent Airfare Dashboard

A clean, minimal dashboard presenting important airfare insights at a glance.

### 🤖 AI-Based Price Prediction

Analyzes historical and contextual data to estimate future airfare trends.

### 🚨 Anomaly Detection

Identifies unusual airfare movements and highlights prices that significantly deviate from expected patterns.

### 🔔 Price Alerts

Users can identify significant fare changes and receive actionable price alerts.

### ⛽ Fuel Price Intelligence

Tracks fuel-price movements and analyzes their potential relationship with airfare changes.

### 📈 CPI Comparison

Compares airfare trends against **Consumer Price Index (CPI)** data to understand transportation inflation.

### 🧠 Explainable Insights

Rather than giving users only a prediction, SkyRate provides understandable reasons behind significant fare movements.

### 🌐 Multi-Source Data Analysis

Combines information from multiple aviation, economic, and market data sources to create a broader view of airfare dynamics.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

SkyRate is built around three principles:

**Clarity** — Complex aviation and economic information should be easy to understand.

**Minimalism** — The interface focuses on the information that actually matters.

**Explainability** — Predictions should be accompanied by understandable insights rather than presented as unexplained numbers.

## 📊 Core Intelligence

SkyRate considers multiple signals when analyzing airfare:

| Signal               | Purpose                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| ✈️ Airfare           | Track historical and current fare movement |
